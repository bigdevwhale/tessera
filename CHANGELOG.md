## [1.0.0]

- The same questions, each in its own rhythm: every day (taking turns), every week, every month, and a birthday interview every year.
- Answers on film with the built-in camera: the question stays on screen, last time's frame shows faintly, a 3-2-1 countdown and a time ring. Answers by voice or in words too.
- Each person's year is a mosaic panel, one stone per day. Filmed answers show their frame; gilded stones are kept above all.
- A year ago: the earlier answer comes back right after a new one.
- Through the years: the chorus of years, answers unchanged since…, words of the year, and this day in earlier years.
- A library of questions for little ones, children, teenagers, grown-ups and elders, in English and Russian, plus your own.
- An evening reminder, only when something is waiting. A ZIP backup with all media. No account, no cloud.
